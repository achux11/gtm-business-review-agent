# GTM Business Review

## Headline
- Revenue reached $3.37M in FY2025, up 30.7% reported and 32.8% at constant currency; the model forecasts $4.28M for FY2026 (27.1% growth).
- Only 2 of 30 marketing campaigns produced a statistically significant sales lift, and the portfolio's incremental sales were -$30K versus control, which is indistinguishable from zero.

## Drivers
- Web converts trials at 41.9% versus 29.8% on Android, a 12.1 pts gap, while mobile's share of revenue grew from 44.4% to 49.0%.
- Churn falls from 17.4% in a subscriber's first month to 5.8% by month 12, so early-tenure retention drives the forecast.
- The top 25% of members generate 60.4% of sales, and member retention is 89.7% period to period.

## Risks
- 47.0% of revenue is billed in non-USD currencies (4 currencies); a 10% currency move changes FY2026 revenue by $201K, and currency already cost 2.1 pts of FY2025 growth.
- Short-term run-rate forecasts look fine but miss long-range dynamics: over 24 months the cohort model's error is 2.8% versus 17.6% for a run-rate baseline.

## Recommendations
- Prioritize Android onboarding tests to close the 12.1 pts conversion gap as mobile becomes a larger share of revenue.
- Stop scaling broad campaigns without a control group; fund the 2 that showed lift and re-test the rest.
- Protect the top 25% of members, who drive 60.4% of sales, with retention offers rather than broad discounts.
- Plan FY2026 on constant-currency numbers and show the $201K currency range alongside them.

_Subscription figures come from a simulated business; campaign figures are real retail data._
